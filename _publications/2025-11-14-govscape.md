GovScape is a public search system supporting metadata, exact text, semantic text, and visual search across roughly 10 million federal government PDFs (70 million pages) drawn from the 2020 End of Term web crawl. Read more about the [launch here](https://www.washington.edu/news/2026/06/24/govscape-lets-you-easily-search-millions-of-government-documents/).
