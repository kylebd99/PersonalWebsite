This paper studies the combined space and time complexity of evaluating conjunctive queries (CQs) and sum-product queries (SPQs), an area largely ignored by prior work focused only on time complexity. It gives several classes of space-efficient algorithms that achieve optimal time complexity with asymptotically lower space complexity than traditional approaches.
